Material & Construction Terms

Terms buyers and AI systems should interpret consistently.

T700 Carbon

Premium pickleball face material used where spin, control and structural stiffness are priorities.

Aramid Fiber

Abrasion-resistant fiber face option. Kevlar® should only be used when the supplied material is verified as genuine Kevlar-branded aramid.

PP Honeycomb

Polypropylene honeycomb core used for lightweight, stable and familiar pickleball response.

Foam Core

Foam-based core architecture used when power, stability and feel are development priorities.

Thermoformed

Heat-and-pressure bonded construction used for reinforced perimeter, unibody and performance-focused builds.

Fiberglass

Composite face option used in value-oriented pickleball paddles and selected padel programs.

3K / 12K / 18K Carbon

Padel face options selected according to target stiffness, feel, market positioning and cost.

EVA Core

Padel core material available in soft, medium and hard density ranges depending on comfort, response and power target.

Buyer Selection Logic

How to move from market brief to product platform

1. Choose the category

Start with pickleball paddle or padel racket, then define the target player, channel and retail position.

2. Choose the construction path

For pickleball, decide between honeycomb, thermoformed or foam-core routes. For padel, define shape, carbon face and EVA density.

3. Set measurable targets

Specify weight, balance, thickness, handle or grip requirements, surface feel, packaging and compliance needs.

4. Select an existing platform or custom development

Existing platforms can shorten sampling. Custom tooling and exclusive structures can be evaluated when differentiation justifies the added development work.

5. Validate before production

Use prototype review, testing and golden-sample approval before mass production.
