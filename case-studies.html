Turnkey OEM Project Milestones (4-Stage Execution)

Stage 1: Engineering & CAD (Day 1-3)

Target weight (220g) & balance calculation, honeycomb density selection, and 3D artwork mapping.

Stage 2: Fast Sampling (Day 4-8)

Prototype molding, edge tape bonding, in-house deflection pre-testing, and express courier to client.

Stage 3: Lab Testing (Day 9-25)

Sample submission to Element U.S. Space & Defense laboratory; 100% test pass on first submission.

Stage 4: Bulk Production (Day 26-45)

BOM locked mass production with custom retail packaging, strict QC checks, and international export.
