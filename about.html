Carbonova Sports

A B2B sports-manufacturing brand focused on advanced pickleball paddles, OEM development and private-label production.

Our Position

Technology-forward presentation. Factory-backed delivery.

Carbonova Sports is the international sports brand of Xiamen Carbon Rock Material Technology Co., Ltd.. The brand was created to give global buyers a clearer, more modern way to evaluate carbon-fiber paddle technology, customization options and manufacturing capability.

Our focus is B2B: helping brands, distributors, importers and sports companies develop products that fit their target market rather than simply choosing from a generic catalog.

Core Capabilities
  • T700, 3K, 12K and 18K carbon options
  • Thermoformed paddle programs
  • OEM / ODM and private label
  • Prototype development
  • USA Pickleball-approved product experience
  • Export and global customer experience
