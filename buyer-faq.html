OEM Buyer FAQ: Pickleball Paddles & Padel Rackets

Short, practical answers for brands, distributors and importers comparing suppliers, materials, MOQ, samples, compliance and private-label development.

Orders & Development

MOQ, samples and project timing

What is the MOQ for a private-label program?

MOQ depends on construction, artwork process, packaging, tooling and customization level. A typical private-label program may start around 50–100 pieces on selected platforms, while simpler or more complex projects can differ.

Can you make samples before mass production?

Yes. OEM/ODM programs can include prototype sampling, revision and golden-sample approval before mass production.

How long do sampling and production take?

Timing depends on whether the project uses an existing platform or new tooling, material and artwork requirements, testing needs, packaging and order volume. We confirm the timeline after requirement review rather than quoting one fixed lead time for every program.

Can you develop a custom mold or custom shape?

Custom shapes and tooling can be evaluated when required. Tooling scope, cost and minimum volume are confirmed after reviewing drawings, reference products or target specifications.

Materials

Pickleball and padel construction options

What pickleball paddle materials are available?

Development options include fiberglass, T700 carbon, aramid fiber, thermoformed construction, polypropylene honeycomb and foam-core structures. The final choice should match your target performance, price point and market position.

What padel racket materials and cores are available?

Padel programs can use fiberglass, 3K, 12K and 18K carbon faces, round, teardrop and diamond shapes, plus soft, medium and hard EVA core options.

Can materials be mixed across a product range?

Yes, subject to engineering review. Brands often use different materials, shapes and finishes to separate entry, mid-range and performance tiers while keeping a consistent visual identity.

Branding & Packaging

Private label options

Can Carbonova add our logo and graphics?

Yes. Programs can include logo placement, graphics, colorways, surface finish, grip details and other visible brand elements.

Can you supply retail packaging?

Yes. Options can include boxes, covers, inserts, barcode labels, accessories and shipping cartons. Packaging details are confirmed with the product specification.

Certification & Verification

What buyers should verify before launch

Are Carbonova pickleball paddles USA Pickleball approved?

Approval is model-specific. Carbonova has paddle certification testing experience and can support testing requirements, but buyers should verify the exact model's current approval status before marketing it as approved.

What quality or compliance information can buyers review?

The site provides factory and quality information including ISO 9001:2015, amfori BSCI, CE verification information and USA Pickleball paddle testing references. Project-specific compliance requirements should be confirmed for the exact product and target market.

How can we verify the factory?

Review the Manufacturer Profile, Factory Capability and Quality & Certifications. Buyers can also request a factory video introduction, physical samples or a project-specific inspection approach.

Useful inputs include target country and sales channel, estimated first order and annual volume, target retail or sourcing price, reference product, preferred materials, performance targets, artwork and packaging needs, compliance requirements and planned launch timing.
