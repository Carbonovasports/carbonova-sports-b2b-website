3K Carbon Fiber Pickleball Paddle OEM Platform

Premium 3K woven carbon fiber face engineered with a perimeter foam-injected PP honeycomb core for optimal ball dwell time, crisp pop, and high torsional stability. Compare our T700 Raw Carbon and GEN5 Foam Core platforms before requesting a customized OEM quotation.

Speed + Torsional Stability

3K Woven Carbon Fiber + Perimeter Foam Core

Designed for competitive tournament brands, this platform pairs a structured 3K woven carbon fiber face with high-density PP honeycomb and perimeter foam injection. The resulting dynamics achieve a low swing weight (≤ 114) for fast kitchen battles paired with an elevated twist weight (> 6.5) for expansive off-center forgiveness.

  • Aerospace 3K Woven Carbon: Cross-woven carbon weave delivers enhanced surface tactile friction and uniform ball deflection.
  • Foam-Injected Perimeter: Foam core perimeter dampens harsh vibration and widens the sweet spot.
  • Custom Hardware Finishing: TPU protective edge tape with brand logo, branded molded butt cap, and custom perforated cushioned grip.
  • Tournament Dynamics: Fast swing weight (≤ 114) with high torsional resistance (twist weight > 6.5).
  • Agile OEM Manufacturing: Custom shapes, thicknesses, UV print graphics, and rapid delivery in under 25 days.

Competitive Advantages

Why Brands Choose the 3K Carbon Platform

Material Stiffness & Spin

Structured 3K Weave

The 3K bidirectional weave structure provides heightened torsional stiffness and crisp tactile feedback on dinks and drives, delivering lasting surface spin without premature grittiness degradation.

Dampening & Forgiveness

Perimeter Foam Edge

High-density foam injected around the honeycomb perimeter expands the effective sweet spot, dampens unwanted harmonic vibrations, and prevents edge-delamination under heavy play.

Speed & Agility

Low Swing Weight (≤ 114)

Engineered mass distribution achieves an agile swing weight (≤ 114 kg·cm² tournament rated) for lightning-fast kitchen hand speed, paired with a high twist weight (> 6.5) for rock-solid stability.
