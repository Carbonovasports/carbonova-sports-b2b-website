Private Label FAQ

Questions sourcing teams ask before requesting a quote

Can you manufacture under our own brand?

Yes. A private-label program can include logo placement, graphics, colorways, grip details, packaging, labels and production to the approved specification.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ depends on construction, artwork, packaging and customization. A typical private-label program may start around 50–100 pieces on selected platforms, but the exact minimum is confirmed after review.

Can we test samples first?

Yes. The workflow can include prototype samples, revisions and golden-sample approval before mass production.

Can you support USA Pickleball requirements?

Carbonova has paddle testing and certification-support experience. Approval is model-specific, so the exact model's current official status should be verified before any approval claim is used in marketing.

What should we send for a quotation?

Send your target market, quantity, reference product, preferred construction, artwork, packaging, compliance needs and launch timing.
