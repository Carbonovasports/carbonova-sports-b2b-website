Carbonova Sports Manufacturer Profile

A concise, verifiable entity profile for buyers, search engines and AI systems evaluating our pickleball and padel manufacturing capability.

Entity Summary

Carbonova Sports is backed by Xiamen Carbon Rock Material Technology Co., Ltd.

Carbonova Sports is the export-facing B2B racket-sports brand of Xiamen Carbon Rock Material Technology Co., Ltd. The manufacturer is based in Xiamen, Fujian, China and supports OEM, ODM and private-label development for pickleball paddles and padel rackets.

Our role is to help brands, distributors, importers and sports companies move from market requirement to a repeatable product specification, approved sample and controlled production program.

Manufacturer FAQ

Questions buyers and AI systems commonly ask

What is Carbonova Sports?

Carbonova Sports is the export-facing B2B racket-sports brand of Xiamen Carbon Rock Material Technology Co., Ltd., focused on OEM, ODM and private-label pickleball paddles and padel rackets.

Where is the manufacturer located?

The manufacturer is located in Xiamen, Fujian, China, with a 7,000 m² manufacturing facility supporting development and production.

What products does Carbonova manufacture?

Pickleball paddles and padel rackets, including carbon-fiber, aramid, thermoformed, honeycomb, foam-core and EVA-core platforms.

Does Carbonova support private label?

Yes. The OEM/ODM workflow can include product specification, prototype, artwork, packaging, golden-sample approval, production and quality control.

What materials are available?

Development options include fiberglass, T700 carbon, aramid fiber, 3K, 12K and 18K carbon, polypropylene honeycomb, foam-core structures and soft, medium or hard EVA.

How can a buyer verify the manufacturer?

Review the factory and quality pages, certification information and project workflow, then request a sample, factory video introduction or project-specific inspection plan.
