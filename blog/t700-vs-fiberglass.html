T700 Carbon vs Fiberglass for Private Label Paddles

How to align material choice with target customer, price point and product story.

T700 raw carbon and fiberglass are not simply “better” or “worse.” They usually serve different commercial positions.

T700 raw carbon

T700 carbon is commonly selected for premium control and spin-oriented paddles. It gives brands a strong technical story, recognizable surface appearance and a path into higher retail price points.

Typical strengths

  • Premium technical positioning
  • Control and spin-focused messaging
  • Suitable for advanced or committed players
  • Works well with 14 mm and 16 mm platforms

Fiberglass and composite

Fiberglass can be useful for beginner paddles, club programs, promotional sets and price-sensitive channels. It also provides flexibility for colorful graphics.

Typical strengths

  • Accessible target cost
  • Flexible visual design
  • Good for sets and entry programs
  • Useful for testing a new market

How buyers should choose

Start with the target retail price, player profile, competitive references, order volume and required margin. Material should support the commercial plan, not replace it.
