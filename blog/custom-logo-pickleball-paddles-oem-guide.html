2. Preserving Friction: How We Balance Custom Logos with Raw Carbon Spin

The biggest concern for performance brands is that heavy ink layers can smooth out raw carbon fiber texture, reducing spin rates below competitive levels.

Carbonova uses a proprietary Micro-Droplet UV Texturing Process:

  • Open Carbon Weave Zones: The core hitting area preserves exposed Toray T700 raw carbon fibers, maintaining natural abrasive grip (RPM > 2000).
  • Flexible Matte Ink Bonding: High-adhesion UV curable inks flex under ball impact without flaking, delaminating, or filling carbon micro-valleys.
  • USAPA Compliance: All printing setups undergo strict surface roughness (Ra < 40 µm) and glossiness testing per USA Pickleball tournament standards.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Can we mix multiple colorways or designs within a 50-piece order?

Yes. On standard platforms sharing the same mold and edge guard color, you can split 50 pieces across 2 graphic variants (e.g., 25 units in Black/Gold, 25 units in White/Teal) to test market demand.

Q2: What vector formats are required for 3D mockup generation?

We accept Adobe Illustrator (.AI), PDF, EPS, and high-resolution PNG files (300 DPI with transparent background). Our design team can also assist with vectorization and Pantone color matching free of charge.
