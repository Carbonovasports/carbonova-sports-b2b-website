Pickleball Paddle MOQ & OEM Price Guide

A practical sourcing framework for brands, distributors, wholesalers and clubs comparing pickleball paddle factories, private-label programs and custom development.

Buyer Decision Summary

What should you compare before requesting a quote?

Compare the same construction, quantity, artwork, packaging and shipping terms across suppliers. Carbonova's current site presents a 50-piece trial MOQ on selected platforms; the final MOQ, sample plan, quotation and compliance route must be confirmed for your exact project.

1. MOQ: define the program before comparing factories

MOQ is not one universal number. It applies to a defined combination of platform, shape, face material, graphics, grip, packaging and quantity split. A stock platform with brand artwork can follow a different minimum from a new mold or a fully custom construction.

Carbonova's current product and OEM pages present a 50-piece trial MOQ on selected platforms. Treat that as a starting point for a project discussion, not as a blanket promise for every model. Ask the factory to confirm:

  • MOQ per model, artwork and colorway;
  • whether artwork can be split within one construction program;
  • whether custom tooling, packaging or special materials create a higher minimum;
  • which quantity breaks are available for the same approved specification.

For a platform overview, compare the pickleball paddle catalog with the OEM and private-label program.

2. Price: compare quote inputs, not isolated unit numbers

A useful OEM price comparison starts with an identical brief. The factory should identify what is included and what remains project-dependent.

Quote variableWhat the buyer should confirm
ConstructionFace material, core, thickness, shape, weight and balance target.
BrandingArtwork process, surface finish, grip, edge guard and end-cap treatment.
PackagingRetail box, inserts, barcode labels, carry bag and master-carton requirements.
Commercial termsQuantity, sample charges, tooling, inspection, Incoterm and freight destination.

There is no responsible fixed price on this guide. For technical options, review T700 Raw Carbon, GEN5 Foam Core and Custom OEM Molds, then ask for a quotation based on one written specification.

3. Samples: approve the right reference before production

A sample is useful only when the approval criteria are clear. Before requesting one, define the intended player, market position, target retail price, preferred construction, weight range, artwork and packaging level.

  • Use a platform sample when you are testing market fit and branding.
  • Use a development sample when shape, layup, core or balance needs engineering changes.
  • Record the approved face material, dimensions, weight, grip and graphics before bulk production.
  • Confirm sample cost, shipping, revision scope and timing for the selected platform in writing.

Send the brief through the RFQ and sample request page. The current contact flow asks for market, quantity, product interest, target price, launch timing, reference link and project details.

4. Specifications: turn a product idea into a comparable brief

Use this checklist before asking multiple factories to quote. Missing fields create false price comparisons and revision delays.

Performance and construction

  • Shape and overall dimensions
  • Core material and thickness
  • Face material and surface finish
  • Target weight, balance and grip length
  • Edge guard, foam or perimeter treatment

Brand and delivery

  • Logo artwork and color references
  • Grip, end cap and accessory details
  • Packaging and labeling requirements
  • Target quantity and market
  • Testing or compliance route to confirm

For construction education, read the thermoformed paddle guide and the OEM manufacturer sourcing guide. They explain why material, structure and quality controls should be written into the specification.

5. Factory verification: check evidence that affects your order

A polished catalog is not enough to evaluate a B2B supplier. Verify the legal entity, factory capability, production process, quality controls, sample handling and the certification path for the exact product design.

  • Review the factory capability page for production and process information.
  • Review the quality page for quality-system and testing information.
  • Ask which operations are performed in-house and which are outsourced.
  • Request the inspection points for materials, weight, balance, appearance and packaging.
  • Confirm whether an existing test or certification route applies to your final shape, materials and surface.

Do not treat a general company certificate or an earlier product test as automatic approval for a new design. The project-specific route must be confirmed before production.

6. Sourcing workflow: from brief to RFQ

  1. Define the market. Identify target player, channel, retail position and expected quantity.
  2. Select a platform. Start with a proven construction before considering custom tooling.
  3. Prepare the brief. Include materials, dimensions, weight, artwork, packaging and compliance questions.
  4. Request comparable quotes. Ask suppliers to separate sample, tooling, unit, packaging, inspection and freight items.
  5. Approve the sample. Record the final specification and revision decisions.
  6. Confirm production controls. Align the approved BOM, inspection checkpoints and shipping terms before ordering.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does MOQ mean for a pickleball paddle OEM program?

MOQ is the minimum quantity for a defined production program. Carbonova's current site presents a 50-piece trial MOQ on selected platforms, but the exact MOQ must be confirmed for the construction, artwork, packaging and order split.

Can I get a fixed pickleball paddle price from this guide?

No. A responsible OEM quotation depends on the platform, face material, core, shape, weight, graphics, packaging, quantity and shipping terms. Use this guide to prepare a comparable brief, then request a project-specific quotation.

What should I include when requesting a pickleball paddle sample?

Include the target market, platform or reference paddle, face material, core thickness, shape, weight range, grip, artwork, packaging, quantity and required compliance route. The factory can then confirm open specifications before sampling.

How should I verify a pickleball paddle factory?

Review the factory identity, production capability, quality process, material traceability, sample-to-production controls and certification path for the exact design. Use the factory and quality pages as part of your comparison, not as a substitute for project confirmation.

Ready to prepare a comparable RFQ?

Send your target market, quantity, reference paddle, preferred materials, artwork and packaging requirements. Carbonova can confirm the appropriate platform and the open project variables.
