What should be specified before a sample?

Short answer

Confirm shape, face, EVA, weight, balance and surface direction.

Technical explanation

Round, teardrop and diamond routes lead to different product positions; 3K, 12K, 18K carbon and fiberglass are visible development options, alongside soft, medium and hard EVA.
