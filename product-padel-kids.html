Kids & Junior Padel Racket Platform

Ultra-lightweight 300–320g youth engineering featuring an arm-safe Soft EVA 12–15D Core, Toray T700 unibody tubular frame, and compact 420–430mm geometry tailored for junior padel academies, clubs and custom brand lines.
