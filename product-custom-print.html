Customization Capabilities

5 Tailored Hardware & Graphic Zones

Every element of the paddle can be branded and customized to match your company or private label visual identity.

🎨 1. UV Printed Image & Text

High-resolution full-face digital UV printing supporting multi-color gradients, custom character illustrations, corporate branding, and micro-matte friction texture.

🔘 2. Custom Silicone Collar Ring

Flexible silicone neck ring featuring your molded brand name or logo, securing the grip tape finish with clean aesthetics and premium hand feel.

💎 3. Crystal Logo on Butt Cap

High-transparency 3D dome resin crystal logo embedded directly into the ergonomic molded butt cap for high-end retail differentiation.

🛡️ 4. Custom Guard Tape & Color

Durable white, black, or metallic golden TPU protective edge tape with optional continuous brand text or logo printing to prevent court abrasion.

🎾 5. Custom Perforated Grip

Moisture-wicking, non-slip perforated cushioned grip wrap available in classic brown leather style, sleek black, or custom pantone colors.
