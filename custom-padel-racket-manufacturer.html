Padel Buyer FAQ

Questions to resolve before sampling

Can you manufacture under our brand?

Yes. Private-label scope can include logo, full graphics, colorways, surface finish, grip details, packaging and labels.

Which shape should we choose?

Round is generally used for control and a larger sweet spot, teardrop for balanced performance, and diamond for power-oriented positioning. The final recommendation should also consider target weight, balance and player profile.

Which carbon weave should we select?

3K, 12K and 18K carbon can be evaluated according to desired stiffness, feel, price point and marketing position. Fiberglass is also available for selected value-oriented programs.

Can EVA density be changed?

Yes. Soft, medium and hard EVA routes can be considered according to comfort, response and power targets.

Can we approve a sample before production?

Yes. Prototype review, revision and golden-sample approval are part of the available OEM workflow.
