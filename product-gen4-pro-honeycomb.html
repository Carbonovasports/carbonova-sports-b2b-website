Core Engineering & Sourcing FAQ

PP Honeycomb Core Construction & OEM Development

Key technical specifications for tournament-grade private label pickleball paddle programs.

1. Why choose PP Honeycomb Core Construction for Tournament Pickleball Paddles?

Polypropylene (PP) Honeycomb Core Construction offers the highest fatigue resistance and dynamic shock absorption in competitive pickleball. In the GEN4 PRO Series, we fuse structural PP honeycomb with surrounding perimeter MPP / EVA dampening foam and authentic Japanese Toray T700 raw carbon fiber under multi-stage heat press. This unibody construction eliminates dead spots, expands the sweet spot by 25%, and ensures USA Pickleball standard compliance without core delamination.

2. What custom branding and packaging options are supported for PP Honeycomb paddles?

We support full private-label customization from 50 pieces: including custom gradient edge guards, molded silicone end caps with your logo, premium PU grip wraps, textured UV face printing, and custom retail gift boxes with laser-cut EVA foam inserts ready for Amazon FBA or pro-shop retail.
