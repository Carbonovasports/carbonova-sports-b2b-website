Engineering FAQ: Custom Pickleball Paddle With Packing Box

Technical guidance for brand founders, corporate gifting buyers, and sports retail distributors.

1. What custom packaging box styles and inserts are available for OEM pickleball paddle sets?

Carbonova offers full turnkey luxury packaging customization, including magnetic closure rigid gift boxes, carbon-texture embossed rigid boxes, drawer slide-out boxes, and corrugated retail display boxes. Inserts feature precision laser-cut high-density EVA foam or eco-friendly molded pulp tailored to hold 1-2 paddles, tournament balls, neoprene covers, overgrip tapes, and carbon erasers. Learn more in our Custom Logo & Packaging Branding Guide.

2. What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) and lead time for custom pickleball paddles with custom boxes?

We support new brand incubation with a low Trial MOQ of 50 sets per design, including both fully customized paddles and custom printed packing boxes. Prototyping takes 4 to 7 business days with free 24-hour 3D vector mockups, and agile mass production is dispatched within < 25 days. Review our Pickleball Paddle MOQ & Production Guide.

3. Can we customize the paddle face material, core thickness, and handle length inside the set?

Yes. You can select Japanese Toray T700/T800 raw carbon fiber, aramid (Kevlar), or titanium composite faces, paired with GEN5 TFP/MPP multi-layer foam cores or high-density PP honeycomb cores in 13mm, 14mm, or 16mm thicknesses. Extended 140mm-150mm handles and custom gradient edge guards are fully supported. Compare carbon weaves in our T700 vs. T800 Carbon Fiber Guide.

4. Do your custom packaging sets comply with Amazon FBA and international retail drop-test standards?

Yes. All custom magnetic gift boxes and internal high-density EVA trays are engineered to pass ISTA 1A / Amazon FBA drop-testing standards, ensuring zero transit damage during international air/sea freight and direct-to-consumer parcel shipping.
